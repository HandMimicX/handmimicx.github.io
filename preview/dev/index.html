Overview

Learning skills through observation.

HandMimicX explores learning dexterous manipulation skills from human videos. The framework converts observed hand–object interactions into robot demonstrations and uses the resulting data to train a visuomotor policy. By combining interaction point clouds with robot proprioceptive states, the policy connects visual observations and hand motion to executable control actions.

Approach

From video to manipulation.

Observe interactions

Recover hand and object motion from human demonstrations to capture how the hand interacts with its environment.

Construct demonstrations

Transfer human interaction priors to the robot and spatially augment the resulting demonstrations for policy learning.

Learn robot actions

Train a visuomotor policy conditioned on interaction point clouds and proprioceptive states to perform dexterous manipulation.
